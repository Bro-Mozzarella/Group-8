03/ LOOK CLOSER

OUR RESEARCH

Gaming Content & Teen Interests

We researched why teenagers play video games and what they enjoy about gaming. A 2024 Pew Research Center survey found that 85% of U.S. teens play video games, while 87% of teen players said fun or entertainment is a reason they play. The research also found that 72% play to spend time with others, showing that gaming can be both entertaining and social.

Viewer Interaction

Livestreams allow viewers to participate in real time through comments, questions, reactions, and other interactive features. This can make the audience feel more connected to the creator instead of simply watching passively. For Pixel Blooms, viewers could share game suggestions, react to gameplay, answer questions, and participate in challenges.
